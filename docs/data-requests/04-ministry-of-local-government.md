## Request for copies of the instruments that fix the Vungu Rural District Council area and classify land within it

**1. Why we are writing.** The council's digital land register has to say, for every parcel, what category of land it is and on what authority. It also has to know exactly what the council area is. We have the council's working records but not, in every case, the instruments behind them, and we would rather cite the instrument than rely on memory.

**2. What we ask for.**

1. The instrument that established or last altered the Vungu Rural District Council area, with its Gazette reference and any description or map attached to it, and the list of wards it comprises (the division into wards is a matter under section 8 of the Rural District Councils Act [Chapter 29:13]).
2. Copies of the Gazette notices and statutory instruments that declare or classify land in the council area, for example as urban council land, communal land, resettlement or other categories under section 3 and related provisions, with the land they refer to.
3. The current model building by-laws, or the by-laws applicable to the council, and the model by-laws on which they rest, with their dates.
4. Any circular since [year] on the keeping of land records or on digital records by rural district councils.
5. The Ministry's preferred contact for questions about the boundaries of rural district councils.

**3. Form.** Scanned copies are sufficient. Where a map or description is attached, please include it. Where the instrument exists in digital form we would be grateful for that.

**4. How we will use it.** We will record the instrument's reference against each parcel or area that depends on it, so that a land category in the register can be traced to a notice. Where we cannot find an instrument for a land category the council has been using, we will say "the council's own record" and tell the Ministry which parcels are affected.

**5. Timing.** We would be grateful for a reply by [date, suggest 30 days].

**6. Contact.** [Name, post, telephone, email].
