**Subject: What the land register needs from the finance records**

1. The land register shows what a stand is, not what it owes. Three things in the council's work depend on finance information: the rates and levies on a stand, the clearance certificate required before transfer (Rural District Councils Act, section 109), and the charges the council fixes by resolution (section 76). Today none of these is linked to a stand in the register.
2. Please let me know: (a) which system holds the rates and billing records, and who supplies it; (b) whether accounts are identified by stand number, by owner or by account number only; (c) whether the council holds a valuation roll, and its date; (d) whether a statement of arrears per stand can be produced as a file.
3. Please say who should be the contact for the supplier, and whether the contract restricts the council from exporting its own data.

## Part B: letter to the supplier

[Date]
Our ref: [reference]

[Supplier name and address]

Dear Sir or Madam

### Request for an export and an interface from the council's finance system

**1. Why we are writing.** Vungu Rural District Council is linking its digital land register to its finance records, so that an officer can see, for a stand, whether it is up to date, and so that a clearance certificate can be issued from facts and not from memory. The council owns its data.

**2. What we ask for.**

1. A one-off export of the account list: account number, the stand or property reference as held, the account holder's name, the account status, and the date of the last payment.
2. A statement of whether the system can produce, on request, a payment status for one account: up to date, in arrears, or in dispute, and the date it was true. We ask for the status band, not the amounts.
3. The valuation roll, if it is held in the system, with its date.
4. A description of the system's own data fields and any interface it offers, for example a database view, an export schedule or an API, with the conditions and the cost, if any.

**3. Form.** CSV or Excel for the export. For the interface, a written description.

**4. How we will use it.** The council will use the export to link accounts to parcels by stand reference. Matches that are not exact will be put to a council officer, not made by guesswork. Personal data will be seen only by council finance and land officers.

**5. Timing.** We would be grateful for a reply by [date, suggest 30 days].

**6. Contact.** [Name, post, telephone, email].
